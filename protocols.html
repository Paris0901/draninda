Protocol I · Trauma Recovery Architecture

The MINDWEAVE Framework™

A daily clinical architecture designed to unshackle trauma recovery from passive observation, actively weaving cognitive clarity and somatic regulation into everyday resilience.

Theoretical Foundations

Traditional trauma recovery models often fail during acute dysregulation because they rely heavily on verbal processing when prefrontal executive functions are temporarily compromised. The MINDWEAVE Framework™ establishes an interlocking daily routine that bridges neurobiological grounding with relational safety.

Developed across tertiary psychiatric care settings, this protocol is structured around four interlocking therapeutic pillars that clinicians and survivors can systematically deploy.

The Four Structural Pillars

  • Mindful Neuro-Somatic Grounding: Immediate sensory re-orienting techniques that interrupt hyperarousal and sympathetic nervous system loops within 90 seconds.
  • Cognitive Clarity Scaffolding: Structured self-inquiry protocols that separate acute emotional flashbacks from present-day reality testing.
  • Relational Safety Anchoring: Identifying and mapping interpersonal safe zones, establishing clear boundary communication without triggering attachment panic.
  • Everyday Resilience Integration: Micro-habits and daily environmental modifications that reinforce neural pathways associated with self-agency and calm.

✦ Clinical Integration Guidelines

MINDWEAVE™ is designed for direct integration into psychiatric treatment plans, outpatient group therapy, and institutional wellness programs.

Licensing Scope

Available for adoption by clinical psychology departments, trauma rehabilitation centers, and specialized hospital psychiatric units under institutional license.

Protocol II · Public Health & Survivor Architecture

The Dignity Dialogues™

Reframing survivor coping mechanisms by turning private trauma and "scar stories" into collective narrative capital and structural safety algorithms.

From Private Pain to Narrative Capital

Survivors of domestic abuse and coercive control often experience severe narrative dispossession—their experiences invalidated by social stigma or institutional indifference. The Dignity Dialogues™ protocol provides a clinical and conversational framework that restores narrative ownership.

Rather than treating trauma stories as passive wounds, this methodology treats lived survivor wisdom as crucial intelligence for public health prevention and community healing.

Core Operational Methodologies

  • Silent Safety Scanning Algorithms: Training survivors and clinicians to recognize subtle non-verbal indicators of escalating interpersonal danger before overt violence occurs.
  • Self-Talk Scaffolding: Systematic cognitive restructuring that replaces internalized abuser voice and trauma-bonding guilt with grounded self-advocacy.
  • Narrative Control Protocols: Guidelines for safely sharing testimony in judicial, medical, and media settings without triggering re-traumatization.
  • Community Listening Circles: Peer-facilitated group structures that transform isolated healing into collective resilience and solidarity networks.

✦ Public Health Impact

Implemented as a digital hub and physical speaking series across India, impacting thousands of mental health professionals, legal advocates, and survivors.

Key Innovation

Bridges the gap between clinical psychiatry and grassroots gender justice, establishing dignified communication standards across institutional barriers.

Protocol III · Relational & Neuroendocrine Ethics

The PMDD Partner Button™

Automated relational luteal sync protocols and couples communication scripting designed to eliminate the emotional information gap during severe PMDD cycles.

Solving the Relational Information Gap

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) creates profound neuroendocrine shifts that can lead to acute interpersonal friction, miscommunication, and relational trauma. During luteal phase drops in allopregnanolone, verbal explanation becomes emotionally taxing for the patient.

The PMDD Partner Button™ is a proprietary clinical protocol and communication system that removes the burden of real-time emotional explanation from the symptomatic partner, replacing it with structured, pre-agreed relational cues.

Protocol Mechanics

  • Luteal Phase Synchronization: Automated or standardized notification protocols that alert partners to the onset of neuroendocrine vulnerability windows.
  • De-escalation Communication Scripting: A curated library of pre-scripted phrases for partners that validate biological distress without engaging in cognitive debates.
  • Relational Safety Boundaries: Pre-established agreements on temporary solitude, parenting adjustments, and decision-making deferrals during acute luteal days.
  • Post-Cycle Reconnection Protocols: Structured debriefing templates deployed once hormonal baseline returns, ensuring mutual emotional repair without guilt or lingering resentment.

✦ Couples Therapy Script Sample

Partner Response Template:

"I recognize that your nervous system is navigating a severe PMDD shift right now. I am not going to argue with the distress you are feeling. I am here to handle [specific household task] and give you complete safety and rest until this biological window passes."

Protocol IV · Institutional Safeguarding & Detection

The Healing Justice Toolkit™

Comprehensive safeguarding architecture featuring the Narcissist’s Playbook detection protocol, UN PSEA structural pillars, and proprietary advocacy mnemonics.

The Narcissist’s Playbook™: Clinical Detection Protocol

Coercive control and narcissistic abuse follow highly predictable behavioral architectures whether occurring within intimate partnerships, executive boardrooms, or academic institutions. Dr. Sidhana’s protocol provides organizations and clinicians with a diagnostic matrix to identify abuse before systemic harm occurs.

1. Idealization & Love Bombing

Overwhelming initial validation, rapid boundary erosion, and artificial intimacy designed to manufacture premature trust and dependency.

2. Gaslighting & Reality Erosion

Systematic denial of documented events, shifting goalposts, and covert invalidation that destabilizes the victim's cognitive self-trust and perception.

3. Triangulation & Isolation

Manufacturing rivalries, leveraging third-party opinions (smear campaigns), and severing the victim's independent social or professional support networks.

Protocol V · Global Survivor Intervention

The WINGS Framework™

Global survivor empowerment and Intimate Partner Violence (IPV) intervention architecture designed to build long-term economic, emotional, and systemic resilience.

Beyond Emergency Intervention

While acute crisis intervention saves lives during intimate partner violence (IPV), long-term survivor recovery requires structural scaffolding that prevents re-victimization and economic dependency.

The WINGS Framework™ integrates clinical trauma psychiatry with social empowerment, providing institutions, shelters, and policymakers with a 5-phase roadmap for survivor emancipation.

The 5 Phases of WINGS

  • Wisdom Recognition: Validating the survivor's internal survival strategies and historical resilience as the primary foundation for recovery.
  • Interventional Safety Planning: Multi-layered digital, physical, and legal security protocols tailored to high-risk environments.
  • Narrative Restructuring: Replacing institutional victim labels with empowered authorship of one's future and identity.
  • Grounded Community Reintegration: Connecting survivors with vocational training, financial literacy, and supportive peer solidarity networks.
  • Sustained Leadership & Advocacy: Creating pathways for recovered survivors to become mentors, policy advisors, and community leaders.

✦ Global Alignment

Directly supporting United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 5 (Gender Equality) and Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-being).
