## 1. Executive Summary & Lived Experience Synthesis

**Dr. Aninda Sidhana** is an internationally acclaimed Indian psychiatrist, psychosexual medicine expert, academician, and human rights advocate with over 15 years of clinical and policy experience. Having personally survived two abusive marriages and domestic violence, Dr. Sidhana transformed her personal trauma into a crusade for gender-sensitive mental health care, survivor advocacy, and social taboos destruction.
